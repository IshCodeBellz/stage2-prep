The small print, in plain words

Is it really one payment?

Yes. No subscription, no renewal, no card kept on file for later. You buy a tier and it stays bought, through every attempt you are allowed and however long the gaps between them are — which, under the current rules, can be years.

What is the refund policy?

Fourteen days, no questions, even if you have read every guide. Practice material that needs a hostage policy is not confident about being worth the money.

Do I need Gold or Platinum to use the simulators?

No. Every drill in the standard battery, both modes, the full exam lengths and Day mode are free and will stay free. Locking the practice would make the thing pointless. The one Platinum simulator is the enhanced VSE — the version with the track and the shape sets — which sits alongside the standard VSE rather than replacing it.

Can you guarantee I will pass?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Cut-off scores are set by the operator, are not published, and move. What preparation reliably fixes is pace, method and surprise — which is where most of the avoidable failures are.

I have my assessment in four days.

Then do not buy anything yet. Open the simulators, sit the exam version of Group Bourdon and the vigilance test tonight, and read the two free guides that go with them. If what you find worries you, come back for the rest.
