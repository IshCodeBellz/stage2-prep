Stage 2 test simulators

GTS trainee train driver · assessment 22 September, Watford

VSE task 1 — dot sequence

The dot moves clockwise around the corners. Mark every square where the sequence breaks.

180 squares · target 3 min

VSE task 2 — letter columns

Mark each box where the two columns hold different letters. Order does not matter.

96 boxes · target 3 min 12 s

VSE task 3 — both at once

Four seconds a pair, auto-advancing. The dot sequence is the primary task.

45 pairs · 3 min

WAFV — vigilance

A grey square flashes. Respond the moment it appears darker than usual.

choose 5, 10 or 30 min

ATAVT — perception

A traffic scene flashes for one second. Report which objects you saw.

20 scenes · about 6 min

2-Hand — co-ordination

Left thumb moves it sideways, right thumb moves it up and down. Keep it inside the channel.

6 runs · buzzer on contact

M7-SJT — ranking

Put four responses in order of preference. 45 seconds a situation, auto-advancing.

8 situations · 6 min

SJE — rating

Rate each response 1 to 5. Checks your answers against each other for contradictions.

6 scenarios · untimed

Day mode

Every drill back to back in one sitting, at full length. Rehearses the fatigue, not just the tasks.

about 1 hr 15 · do this twice before the day
Run these on the largest screen you have. The real assessments are on a desktop with a keyboard, and a phone flatters your scores on the two visual search tasks.
